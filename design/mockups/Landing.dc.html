Features

Booking that respects how meetings really work

Only rooms that fit

Filter by date, time, headcount and equipment. A room for eight never shows up for a meeting of twelve.

No double bookings

Two people cannot take the same slot. The second person sees it is gone before they confirm, and gets the next free options.

Repeating meetings that behave

Book every Monday for eight weeks, then change one date or the whole series without breaking the rest.

Rooms that free themselves

If nobody checks in within 10 minutes, the room goes back to the pool and the waitlist gets the first turn.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

What stops two people booking the same room?

The system checks the time range at the moment you confirm and blocks any overlap. If someone else got there first, you are shown the next free options instead.

Can I book a meeting that repeats?

Yes. Choose daily, weekly or a custom pattern. Later you can change a single meeting or the whole series.

What happens if nobody turns up?

Ten minutes after the start time, an unchecked booking is released so the next person or the waitlist can use the room.

How long is my slot held while I book?

Five minutes. If you do not confirm in that time, the slot goes back to everyone.

Can I limit who books which room?

Admins can restrict a room by team and by hours. For example, the boardroom can be kept for leadership meetings on Mondays.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The search and booking screens are laid out for small screens as well as desktops.
